About this video

Thanks to Epic 4x4 Quest and ORI Struts making this race in Moab happen! Without Nathan Livingston at Epic 4X4 Quest, and his exceptional racing knowledge, we wouldn't have pulled this race off. Check out their YouTube: Overall standing was 9th place. We are pretty happy, considering we had transfer case issues. First problem was user error, we should have been running a different gear ratio, so our low gear was too low for any speed and our high gear was too high, causing the transmission to over heat in this terrain. Also there were issues shifting. If you see us stop on course a couple times it was because of shifting issues. We also lost a tire, slowing us down significantly. So we took it easy, held the car together and got a 9th place position. Not bad for being only our 3rd race ever.
