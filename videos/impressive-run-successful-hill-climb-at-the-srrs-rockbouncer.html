About this video

Digging through the archives of our rockbouncer footage from the Mid-America Southern Rock Racing Series Event in 2020. Watch this solid run in a rockbouncer. This hill made many other buggies flip. The video never does justice to how steep and difficult these climbs are. Especially in the dust! As drivers and racers ourselves we love seeing successful climbs, the drivers put their lives and livelihoods at stake to put on a show for everyone. As much as it's fun to see a crash compilation we know that the successful climbs are more important to the driver. So here's to the climb of the day!
