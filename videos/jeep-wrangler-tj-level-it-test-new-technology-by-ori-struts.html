Testing the pneumatic LEVEL-IT by ORI Struts. The LEVEL-IT automatically stiffens on the load side of this Jeep TJ by blocking the external reservoir, decreasing the compressible nitrogen volume and stiffening the spring rate. This is accomplished without the aid of electronic devices.
