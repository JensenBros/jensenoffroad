About this video

King of the Hammers 2022. Our very first KOH race in Johnson Valley. This race was an absolute blast! Lots of hard work getting the car ready, and we couldn't have done it without ORI Struts, Rugged Radios, RCV axles, Maxxis Tires, Raceline Wheels, and Point 1 Offroad racing giving us pit support. Started the day poorly with a 4 hour late start (bad fuel), but the event staff worked closely with us to at least keep us in the race. Didn't finish, but we did drive the car back onto the trailer after the race. We will definitely return next year.
