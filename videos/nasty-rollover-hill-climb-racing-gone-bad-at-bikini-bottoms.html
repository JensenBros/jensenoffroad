About this video

Formula Offroad is a Motorsport originated in Iceland back in 1965. The sport consumes of 4x4 custom made machines that can climb up vertical hills and take on any obstacle that is put in their way. In Iceland alone are over 30 drivers that are competing today in three classes. Unlimited, Modified and Street Legal.
