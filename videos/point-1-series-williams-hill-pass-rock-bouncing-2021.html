About this video

Check out the very first Point 1 Rock Bouncing and UTV race at the Williams Hill Pass OHV park in Illinois! These guys put on a great show, with some of the best drivers in the rock bouncing and UTV racing circuit. Point 1 brings a mix of short course style and traditional rock bouncer racing into one for a fast paced and adreneline filled experience for both driver and spectator. Watch as these drivers tackle unique and challenging terrain, breaking new ground as they test their metal against one another.
