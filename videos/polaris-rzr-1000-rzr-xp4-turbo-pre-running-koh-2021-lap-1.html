About this video

Check out our pre-running video this year at the 2021 King of the Hammers! This is lap 1 (desert loop with cougar buttes). We came to support the 4858 and 911 (4400 class) cars this year, and they though it best we go pre-run the course to get better idea what was out there before tuning the ORI struts on their cars. Pre-running is a blast, even if it wasn't record time, at least the cars didn't break, and at one point we did go the wrong way. Hopefully we'll be back soon to race!
