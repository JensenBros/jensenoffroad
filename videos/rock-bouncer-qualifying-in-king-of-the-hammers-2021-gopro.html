About this video

Kevin Taylor and Heath Day qualifying at the 2021 King of the Hammers in their Porche 911 themed rock bouncer running an LS 6.0 naturally aspirated engine, ORI Integral Reservoir Struts, 43" Super Swamper TSL SX Sticky Tires, and PSC Full Hydro steering. This was their first attempt at this race. They qualified well and completed 2 laps in the main 4400 class race before losing reverse. What an awsome run!
