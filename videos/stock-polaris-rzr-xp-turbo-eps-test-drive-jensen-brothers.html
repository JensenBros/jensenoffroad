About this video

Took the RZR out to the Little Sahara Sand Dunes in Utah for a test drive. We ran the Fox Shocks at 6 notches down from the hardest setting. So about mid range softness. It handles the whoops great but there was some side to side movement and bucking of the rear end when going over jumps. Power slides and hard turns are scary because it was diving into the turns. Tightening the suspension helps prevent the diving but may have to do some more fine tuning to feel comfortable around high speed turns. After getting cofortable with the Fox Shocks we will be installing ORI Struts brand new bolt in struts specifically build for the RZR 1000 and Turbo XP models. Can't wait to feel the comparison.
